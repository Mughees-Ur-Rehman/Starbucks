Starbucks Secret Menu Drinks

Creative, customer-invented drink combinations built from the regular Starbucks menu — not an official printed menu, but a set of customization ideas any barista can build.

The Starbucks secret menu is best understood as a collection of customer-created combinations rather than a fixed official menu. You can order a base drink and request specific customizations, and most secret menu drinks are simply a regular beverage with extra syrups, sauces, or toppings added. Because these aren't recognized names in the Starbucks ordering system, always describe your base drink and each modification clearly.

These Starbucks secret menu drinks have spread through social media, fan communities, and coffee blogs. They let you explore flavors well beyond the printed menu without needing baristas to memorize obscure names.

How to Order a Secret Menu Drink

Instead of asking for an unfamiliar secret-menu name, tell the barista:

  1. The base drink and size (e.g., "a Grande Vanilla Bean Creme Frappuccino")
  2. The syrups or sauces to add (e.g., "add two pumps of raspberry syrup")
  3. Any topping changes (e.g., "top with whipped cream and caramel drizzle")

This approach works at every store because it uses only standard Starbucks ingredients. You can also use the Starbucks mobile app — select the base drink, then add each customization through the app's modifier options before ordering.

Secret Hot Drinks at Starbucks

Some of the most popular hot drinks at the Starbucks secret menu are built from standard espresso drinks with simple syrup and sauce additions:

Dirty Chai Latte

A Chai Tea Latte with one or two shots of espresso added. Warming, spiced, and caffeinated. Order at hot coffee by asking for a Chai Latte with an added espresso shot.

Snickerdoodle Latte

A Cinnamon Dolce Latte® with white mocha sauce added — a sweet, cinnamon-cookie-inspired hot drink. Ask for a Cinnamon Dolce Latte with one pump of white mocha.

Nutella Latte

A Caffè Latte with hazelnut syrup and mocha sauce blended in — a hazelnut-chocolate combination reminiscent of Nutella spread. Ask for a Latte with hazelnut syrup and mocha sauce.

Tuxedo Mocha

Half white mocha sauce and half regular mocha sauce in a standard Caffè Mocha — a creamy, marbled chocolate flavor. Ask for a Caffè Mocha made with half white mocha, half regular mocha.

Honey Bee Latte

A Latte sweetened with honey blend and vanilla syrup, lightly sweet and floral. Ask for a Latte with honey blend and a pump of vanilla.

Cinnamon Roll Latte

A Latte with cinnamon dolce syrup, white mocha sauce, and vanilla syrup — bakery-sweet and warm. Ask for those three additions on a standard Latte.

Secret Menu FAQ

Is the Starbucks secret menu official?

No. Secret-menu drinks are customer-created combinations rather than a separate official Starbucks menu. Baristas may or may not recognize the nicknames.

Can I order a secret menu drink on the Starbucks app?

Yes — use the app's customization section to add the exact syrups, sauces, and toppings listed in the recipe, selecting your base drink first.

Can every secret drink be ordered at every store?

Not always. Ingredients, equipment, and availability can differ by location. Describing the recipe rather than the nickname improves your chances.

Will a secret menu drink cost more?

The base price stays the same; extra syrups or toppings are charged as standard add-ons, typically $0.50–$1.00 per modification. See Starbucks prices for context.

Where can I find calorie info for secret drinks?

Since recipes vary, calories are estimated based on the base drink calories plus each added ingredient. Check the Starbucks calories guide as a starting point.
