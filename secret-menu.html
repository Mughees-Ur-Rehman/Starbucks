Starbucks Secret Menu Drinks

Creative, customer-invented drink combinations built from the regular Starbucks menu — not an official printed menu, but a set of customization ideas any barista can build.

The Starbucks secret menu is best understood as a collection of customer-created combinations rather than a fixed official menu. You can order a base drink and request specific customizations, and most secret menu drinks are simply a regular beverage with extra syrups, sauces, or toppings added.

Popular Secret Menu Drinks

Secret Frappuccino Creations

Try creative flavor combinations by customizing a regular Frappuccino with syrups, sauces, toppings, or milk choices.

Secret Hot Drinks

Hot drinks can be customized with syrups, sauces, milk alternatives, espresso, and toppings.

Secret Cold Drinks

Iced coffee, cold brew, Refreshers, and iced tea can be personalized with flavors and toppings.

Custom Dessert-Style Drinks

Sweet combinations can be created by pairing a suitable base beverage with complementary syrups and toppings.

Chocolate-Inspired Frappuccino

Vanilla Dessert Drink

A vanilla-focused combination can be customized with vanilla flavor and a creamy topping.

Caramel Dessert Drink

Caramel lovers can customize a coffee or creme base with caramel flavor and drizzle.

Strawberry-Inspired Drink

A fruit-forward custom order can combine strawberry flavors with a suitable cold or blended base.

Cotton Candy Frappuccino

A pink, sweet Frappuccino idea built from vanilla bean creme with raspberry and classic syrup.

Butterbeer Frappuccino

A caramel and toffee nut inspired combination blended into a creme-based Frappuccino.

How to Order a Secret Menu Drink

Instead of asking for an unfamiliar secret-menu name, tell the barista the base drink, size, milk choice, syrup or sauce modifications, and toppings you want. This makes the order clearer and easier to prepare, since names for secret drinks are not standardized across stores.

Frappuccino Secret Menu Ideas

For secret Frappuccinos, start with a standard Frappuccino — like a Vanilla Bean or Java Chip — and describe the flavor customizations you want. Availability of ingredients and customization options can vary by store. Secret Menu FAQ

Is the Starbucks secret menu official?

No. Secret-menu drinks are generally customer-created combinations rather than a separate official menu.

Can every secret drink be ordered at every store?

Not always. Ingredients, equipment, and availability can differ by location.

Will a secret menu drink cost more?

Extra syrups, sauces, or toppings are usually charged as standard customization add-ons, so pricing follows normal modifier pricing.
