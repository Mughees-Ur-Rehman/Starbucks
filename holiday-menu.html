Starbucks Holiday Drink Menu

Seasonal, limited-time drink inspiration for the winter holiday season, from peppermint mochas to gingerbread-style lattes.

Starbucks releases a holiday drink menu each year, typically starting in late October or early November. The seasonal lineup has historically featured returning favorites alongside new limited-edition drinks. Because holiday menus change by year and by market, this page focuses on drink types and flavor profiles that have appeared repeatedly across Starbucks holiday seasons — always verify current availability with your local store or the Starbucks app.

Whether you prefer hot lattes, iced drinks, or blended Frappuccinos, the Starbucks holiday drink menu tends to include options in all three formats for most seasonal flavors.

Classic Holiday Drink Flavors

Peppermint Mocha

One of Starbucks’ most beloved holiday drinks, combining espresso, steamed milk, mocha sauce, and peppermint-flavored syrup, topped with whipped cream and dark chocolate curls. Available hot, iced, and as a Frappuccino®. A recurring holiday menu staple for over two decades.

How to order: Available by name at the counter, drive-thru, or on the Starbucks app during the holiday season.

Chestnut Praline Latte

A seasonal latte built with espresso, steamed milk, and chestnut praline syrup, topped with whipped cream and spiced praline crumbs. This warming, nutty drink has been a holiday menu regular since 2014. Available hot, iced, and as a Frappuccino®.

Toasted White Chocolate Mocha

A holiday twist on the classic White Chocolate Mocha — espresso, steamed milk, and toasted white chocolate mocha sauce, topped with whipped cream, candied cranberry, and crispy white pearls. A richer, more festive version of the year-round favorite.

Caramel Brulée Latte

Espresso combined with steamed milk and caramel brulée sauce, topped with whipped cream and caramel brulée topping. A dessert-inspired holiday drink with a warm, caramelized sugar character.

Gingerbread Latte

One of the original Starbucks holiday drinks, the Gingerbread Latte features espresso, steamed milk, and gingerbread-flavored syrup topped with whipped cream and ground nutmeg. Availability has varied by year and market in recent seasons.

Sugar Cookie Almond Milk Latte

A plant-based holiday option — espresso shaken with oat milk, sugar cookie-flavored syrup, and a blend of spices, served iced with festive red and green sprinkles. A more recent addition to the holiday drinks lineup.

Toffee Nut Latte

A warm, buttery-sweet espresso latte with toffee nut syrup and steamed milk — a classic holiday flavor profile that can also be customized into iced and cold brew drinks.

Holiday Frappuccinos

Seasonal flavors like Peppermint Mocha and Chestnut Praline also appear as blended Frappuccino® versions during the holiday season, adding a frozen option for every festive flavor.

Seasonal Availability & Tips

Holiday and seasonal menu items are limited-time offerings, so exact names, recipes, and availability can change by year and by location. Most holiday drinks launch in early November and remain available through January. Here are some practical tips:

  • Check the Starbucks app: The app shows exactly which seasonal drinks are currently available at your local store.
  • Customize year-round: Even outside holiday season, you can often replicate holiday-style flavors by asking for peppermint or toffee nut syrup on a standard latte — ask a barista about current syrup availability.
  • Watch for early access: Starbucks Rewards members sometimes receive early access to holiday menu launches through the app.

Holiday Drinks: Hot, Iced, or Blended?

Most Starbucks holiday flavors are offered in multiple formats. Peppermint Mocha, for example, is available as a hot latte, iced latte, and Peppermint Mocha Frappuccino®. If your preferred holiday drink isn’t listed as iced, ask the barista — many seasonal syrups work in cold beverages too. See the cold drinks menu or hot coffees menu for year-round base options to customize.

Pairing Ideas

Holiday drinks pair well with bakery items from the breakfast & bakery menu, especially butter croissants, seasonal pastries, and cake pops. A Peppermint Mocha or Chestnut Praline Latte pairs particularly well with a Chocolate Croissant.

Holiday Drinks FAQ

When does the Starbucks holiday menu start?

The holiday drink menu typically launches in early-to-mid November each year, though the exact date varies. Starbucks Rewards members sometimes get advance notice through the app.

Is the Peppermint Mocha available all year?

The Peppermint Mocha is a seasonal holiday drink and is not typically available year-round. However, peppermint syrup may be available at some locations for customizing other drinks off-season — check with your local store.

Are holiday drinks available iced?

Yes. Most Starbucks holiday flavors are offered hot, iced, and as a Frappuccino® blended drink during the holiday season.

Can I order holiday drinks on the Starbucks app?

Yes. Seasonal drinks appear in the Starbucks app during the holiday season and can be fully customized and ordered ahead.
