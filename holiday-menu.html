Starbucks Holiday Drink Menu

Seasonal, limited-time drink inspiration for the winter holiday season, from peppermint mochas to gingerbread-style lattes.

Holiday beverages are seasonal and can change from year to year. This page groups holiday-style drink information and helps visitors explore seasonal favorites and their flavor profiles, whether hot, iced, or blended.

Seasonal Flavor Categories

Peppermint-Style Drinks

Mint and chocolate-inspired seasonal combinations, popular as a hot mocha or blended Frappuccino during the holiday period.

Caramel Holiday Drinks

Warm caramel flavors pair well with coffee, brulee sugar, and creamy seasonal beverages.

Gingerbread-Style Drinks

Spiced, bakery-inspired flavors often associated with winter latte menus.

Chestnut-Inspired Drinks

A seasonal, nutty praline flavor profile commonly associated with winter coffee drinks.

Sugar Cookie Drinks

A sweet, vanilla-forward flavor often used in holiday lattes and blended beverages.

Toffee Nut Drinks

A buttery toffee flavor that pairs well with espresso and steamed milk in cooler months.

Holiday Frappuccinos

Seasonal flavor combinations can also appear in blended Frappuccino® beverages when offered.

Holiday Cold Drinks

Seasonal flavors can be adapted into iced or cold beverage combinations when available.

Seasonal Availability

Holiday and seasonal menu items are limited-time offerings, so exact names, recipes, and availability can change by year and by location. Checking with your local store closer to the season is the best way to confirm what's currently on offer.

Pairing Ideas

Holiday drinks pair well with bakery items from the breakfast & bakery menu, especially butter croissants and seasonal pastries.
