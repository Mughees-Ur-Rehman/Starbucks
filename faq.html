Starbucks Menu FAQ

Answers to common questions about Starbucks drinks, menu categories, prices, sizes, and secret menu orders.

The drinks menu can include brewed coffee, espresso beverages, iced drinks, cold brew, tea, Starbucks Refreshers, Frappuccinos, and seasonal beverages.

It is a collection of customer-created drink combinations and customizations rather than a single official menu. See the full secret menu page for popular ideas.

It is usually clearer to explain the base drink and the specific customizations, because secret-menu names may not be recognized consistently across stores.

Yes. Availability, customization options, and prices can vary by store, market, and season.

Visit the Frappuccino menu page for a dedicated overview of coffee-based and creme-based blended drinks.

Prices typically range from around $3.25 for standard brewed coffee up to $6.75+ for specialty seasonal Grande or Venti Frappuccinos, depending on regional taxes and store format.

Yes. Hot breakfast sandwiches, sous-vide egg bites, pastries, and oatmeal are generally available from store open until sold out for the day.

Whole, 2%, nonfat, oat, almond, soy, and coconut milk are commonly available to customize most hot and cold drinks.

Standard sizes are Short, Tall, Grande, Venti, and Trenta (iced only), with exact availability depending on the drink.

This website serves as an independent guide. For strict allergy or medical ingredient details, consult official store baristas or Starbucks' official nutrition guides.

Brewed coffee such as Pike Place Roast is usually the cheapest option, starting around $3.25 for a Short or Tall. See the full price list for every category.

The Caramel Macchiato, Pink Drink, and Iced Brown Sugar Oatmilk Shaken Espresso are consistently among the top sellers. See the most popular Starbucks drinks page.

Trenta (31 oz) is the largest size, available for iced coffee, Refreshers, and iced tea. Venti is the largest hot-drink size at 20 oz. See the Starbucks sizes guide.

Brewed coffee, Americanos, Cold Brew, and water-based Refreshers are typically under 100 calories in a standard build. See the Starbucks calories guide for a full list.

Most hot and cold espresso drinks can be made dairy-free with oat, almond, soy, or coconut milk, and Cold Brew and Refreshers are naturally dairy-free.

No. Prices vary by region, state taxes, and store format — licensed locations such as airports, hotels, or grocery-store kiosks often price slightly higher than standalone cafes.
