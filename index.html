Customization & Dairy Options

Starbucks beverages can be fully tailored. Select from whole milk, 2% milk, nonfat milk, or plant-based alternatives including Oatmilk, Almondmilk, and Soymilk.

🏷️

Sizes & Pricing Structure

Standard drink sizes range from Short (8 oz), Tall (12 oz), Grande (16 oz), to Venti (20 oz hot / 24 oz iced) and Trenta (31 oz iced). Prices scale based on volume and specialty syrups.

🌱

Calorie & Nutritional Data

Calorie estimations reflect standard builds using 2% milk unless specified otherwise. Requesting sugar-free syrups or non-fat milk reduces total overall calorie count.
