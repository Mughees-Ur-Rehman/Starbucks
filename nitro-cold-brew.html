Starbucks Nitro Cold Brew Price & Calories

The Starbucks Nitro Cold Brew is priced at approximately $4.75 for a standard Grande size and contains around 5 Cal using a standard build. Prices and calories vary by store location, size, and customization — requesting sugar-free syrup or a non-dairy milk can lower the total calorie count.

Dietary Info

Low Calorie, Vegan Friendly.

How to Order the Nitro Cold Brew

To order a Starbucks Nitro Cold Brew, simply ask for it by name at the counter or drive-thru, or search for “Nitro Cold Brew” in the Starbucks app. Let the barista know your preferred size, milk choice, and any extra customizations such as more or less syrup, decaf, or added espresso shots.
