Starbucks Breakfast & Bakery Menu

Warm breakfast sandwiches, egg bites, and fresh bakery pastries to pair with your favorite coffee. Browse Starbucks breakfast menu items with prices and calories below.

Starbucks breakfast food is served from store opening until sold out, and includes warm sandwiches, wraps, sous-vide egg bites, and a full bakery case of pastries and muffins. Oatmeal and yogurt options are also available for a lighter morning choice.

Breakfast Ordering Tips

All-Day Breakfast

Warm breakfast sandwiches and egg bites are typically available all day, not just in the morning.

Pair With a Drink

Pair a breakfast item with brewed coffee, an espresso drink, or a cold beverage from the hot coffee or cold drinks menu.

Lighter Options

Oatmeal, yogurt with fruit, and egg white wraps offer lighter alternatives to a full sandwich.
