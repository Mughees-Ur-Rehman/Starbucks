Starbucks Frappuccino® Menu

Blended coffee and creme-based Frappuccino® beverages topped with whipped cream. Browse the Starbucks Frappuccino menu with prices and calories below.

Frappuccino® blended beverages come in two styles: coffee-based (built on real brewed coffee) and creme-based (caffeine-free, built on milk). All Frappuccinos are blended with ice and can be customized with extra syrup, whipped cream, or toppings like caramel drizzle and chocolate chips.

Frappuccino® Tips

Coffee vs Creme

Coffee-based Frappuccinos contain caffeine from real brewed coffee, while creme-based options are caffeine-free and milk-based.

Customization

Ask for extra whipped cream, half sweetness, or a different milk to adjust flavor and calories.

Blended Texture

All Frappuccino® drinks are blended with ice for a smooth, slushy texture and served cold.
