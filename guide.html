Starbucks Menu Guide

Understand sizes, customization, and how to order confidently — including secret menu drinks — with this practical Starbucks ordering guide.

Use this guide to understand the main Starbucks menu categories, sizing, and customization options so you can order more confidently, whether you're picking a classic drink or building your own combination.

Choosing a Drink

Start with the base beverage, then choose a size, milk, flavor modifications, temperature, and toppings where available. Most drinks on the hot coffee and cold drinks menus follow the same basic customization structure.

Sizes & Pricing Structure

Short (8 oz)

The smallest hot-only size, best for a quick, concentrated cup.

Tall (12 oz)

A compact size available on both hot and cold drinks.

Grande (16 oz)

The most popular size, available hot or iced.

Venti (20 oz hot / 24 oz iced)

The largest standard size, with a bigger iced pour than hot.

Trenta (31 oz iced)

Available on select iced coffee, tea, and Refreshers for the largest cold option.

Customization & Dairy Options

Starbucks beverages can be fully tailored. Select from whole milk, 2% milk, nonfat milk, or plant-based alternatives including oatmilk, almondmilk, and soymilk. Extra espresso shots, sugar-free syrups, and light ice are common ways to adjust flavor and calories.

Calorie & Nutritional Notes

Calorie estimations on this site reflect standard builds using 2% milk unless specified otherwise. Requesting sugar-free syrups, non-fat milk, or less whipped cream generally reduces the total calorie count of a drink.

Secret Menu Ordering Tips

Secret menu names are not a universal official menu. Give the barista the exact ingredients and modifications you want rather than relying on a specific secret-menu name — see the full secret menu page for ideas.
