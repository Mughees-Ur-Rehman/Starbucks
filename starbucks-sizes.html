Starbucks Sizes Guide

A quick guide to every Starbucks cup size, from Short to Trenta, and which drinks each size is available for.

Starbucks drink sizes range from the smallest Short cup to the largest Trenta, and not every size is available for every drink. Here is what each size means and when to order it.

Short (8 oz)

The smallest hot-drink size, mostly used for hot coffee and espresso drinks; not listed on the menu board but available on request.

Tall (12 oz)

The smallest size available for most cold drinks and the standard starting size on the printed menu.

Grande (16 oz)

The most popular Starbucks size and the standard reference size used for prices and calories on this site.

Venti (20 oz hot / 24 oz iced)

The largest standard size for hot drinks (20 oz) and a larger iced size (24 oz) for cold beverages.

Trenta (31 oz)

The largest cold-drink size, available for iced coffee, Refreshers, and iced tea (not available for drinks with milk or espresso in some cases).

How Starbucks Pricing Scales by Size

Prices increase with each size step, but the price per ounce usually goes down as you size up, so a Venti is often better value per ounce than a Tall. See the Starbucks menu prices page for a full price list.
