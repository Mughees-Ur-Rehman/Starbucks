Choosing a Cold Drink

Cold Brew vs Iced Coffee

Cold Brew is steeped for hours in cold water for a smoother, less acidic taste, while iced coffee is brewed hot and chilled over ice.

Starbucks Refreshers®

Made with real fruit pieces and available with or without coconutmilk for a lighter, caffeinated fruit drink.

Cold Foam & Toppings

Vanilla sweet cream, cold foam, and flavored syrups can be added to almost any cold beverage.

Trenta Size

Trenta (31 oz) is available on select cold drinks and teas for those who want the largest iced option.
