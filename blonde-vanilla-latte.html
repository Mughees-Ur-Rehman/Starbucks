Starbucks Blonde Vanilla Latte Price & Calories

The Starbucks Blonde Vanilla Latte is priced at approximately $4.85 for a standard Grande size and contains around 250 Cal using a standard build. Prices and calories vary by store location, size, and customization — requesting sugar-free syrup or a non-dairy milk can lower the total calorie count.

Dietary Info

Vegetarian, Customizable Dairy.

How to Order the Blonde Vanilla Latte

To order a Starbucks Blonde Vanilla Latte, simply ask for it by name at the counter or drive-thru, or search for “Blonde Vanilla Latte” in the Starbucks app. Let the barista know your preferred size, milk choice, and any extra customizations such as more or less syrup, decaf, or added espresso shots.
