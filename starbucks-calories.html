Low-Calorie Starbucks Options

Brewed coffee, Americanos, Cold Brew, and Refreshers made with water are typically the lowest-calorie Starbucks drinks, often under 100 calories in a standard build. Frappuccinos and dessert-style drinks tend to be the highest-calorie options on the menu.
