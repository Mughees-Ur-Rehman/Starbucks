What Affects Starbucks Prices?

Drink Size

Prices scale up from Short to Trenta — larger sizes cost more per cup but less per ounce.

Milk & Syrup Add-ons

Non-dairy milk, extra syrup pumps, and flavor shots usually add a small surcharge.

Store Location

Airport kiosks, hotels, and licensed stores (like inside grocery stores) often price slightly higher than standalone cafes.

Extra Espresso Shots

Adding a shot of espresso to any drink typically adds a modest per-shot charge.
