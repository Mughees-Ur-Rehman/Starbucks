Starbucks Turkey Pesto Panini Price & Calories

The Starbucks Turkey Pesto Panini is priced at approximately $7.25 for a standard Grande size and contains around 480 Cal using a standard build. Prices and calories vary by store location, size, and customization — requesting sugar-free syrup or a non-dairy milk can lower the total calorie count.

Dietary Info

High Protein, Warm Food.

How to Order the Turkey Pesto Panini

To order a Starbucks Turkey Pesto Panini, simply ask for it by name at the counter or drive-thru, or search for “Turkey Pesto Panini” in the Starbucks app. Let the barista know your preferred size, milk choice, and any extra customizations such as more or less syrup, decaf, or added espresso shots.
