Starbucks Hot Coffee Menu

Classic brewed coffee and handcrafted espresso beverages made with rich, roasted flavor. Browse Starbucks hot coffee drinks with prices and calories below, all served warm and freshly prepared.

Starbucks hot coffee menu covers everything from simple brewed coffee to layered espresso beverages like lattes, cappuccinos, and macchiatos. Every drink can be customized with your choice of milk, sweetener, and extra espresso shots, and sizes range from Short to Venti depending on the beverage.

How Hot Coffee Sizes Work

Short & Tall

Short (8 oz) and Tall (12 oz) are the most compact hot coffee sizes, best for a quick, strong cup.

Grande & Venti

Grande (16 oz) is the most popular size, while Venti (20 oz hot) suits those who want a larger, longer-lasting drink.

Espresso Shots

Most espresso drinks can be customized with an extra shot (Ristretto, Solo, Doppio, or Triple) for a stronger coffee flavor.

Milk Choices

Whole, 2%, nonfat, oat, almond, soy, and coconut milk are commonly available to customize any hot coffee drink.
