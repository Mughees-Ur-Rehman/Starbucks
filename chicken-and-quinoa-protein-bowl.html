Starbucks Chicken & Quinoa Protein Bowl Price & Calories

The Starbucks Chicken & Quinoa Protein Bowl is priced at approximately $7.45 for a standard Grande size and contains around 390 Cal using a standard build. Prices and calories vary by store location, size, and customization — requesting sugar-free syrup or a non-dairy milk can lower the total calorie count.

Dietary Info

High Protein, Gluten-Free.

How to Order the Chicken & Quinoa Protein Bowl

To order a Starbucks Chicken & Quinoa Protein Bowl, simply ask for it by name at the counter or drive-thru, or search for “Chicken & Quinoa Protein Bowl” in the Starbucks app. Let the barista know your preferred size, milk choice, and any extra customizations such as more or less syrup, decaf, or added espresso shots.
